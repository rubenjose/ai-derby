Privacy

AI Derby collects nothing about the people who visit it. This page says what the site and the AI Derby uploader do with information, and what they don't.

This site

The site is a set of static pages served by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or ads, and has no accounts or forms. GitHub, which serves the pages, may keep server logs under its own privacy statement. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts. A film loads from YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com) only when you press it; from then on YouTube's privacy policy applies.

The AI Derby uploader

AI Derby has a small program that puts the race films on the AI Derby YouTube channel. It is used only by AI Derby's owner, on their own computer, signed in to their own channel. Nobody else signs in to it.

Through Google's sign-in it uses the YouTube Data API to upload films to that channel, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and release times, and read back their status. The sign-in token is kept on the owner's computer only. It is not sent anywhere else, not shared, not sold, and not used for anything but managing the channel's own videos.

AI Derby's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. To withdraw the uploader's access, remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
